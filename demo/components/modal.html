Project information

A native form validates before closing. Escape and backdrop cancellation are separate, observable native requests.

Long authored content

Native top-layer order determines which surface owns interaction. Closing a child restores focus inside its parent.

Each explicit owner disposes only its own cloned elements. The rest of the document keeps its authored styles and content.

Content remains connected while the native dialog is closed, preserving native form values. Disposal is explicit rather than a rendering directive.

A transparent backdrop is still a modal with a blocked background. Use the explicit modeless method for nonmodal interaction.

There are no enter or leave transitions, no hidden focusable controls, and no animation-completion callback pretending to be a Vue hook.

At narrow widths, enlarged text and RTL, the complete native surface scrolls; the footer stays reachable without a custom scrollbar.

Closing this surface never submits an unrelated form.
