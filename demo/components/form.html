Behavior and ownership

Keep native labels, IDs, fieldsets and legends. Form never generates duplicate accessible names or changes custom validity. Native invalid events do not bubble: observe the native control or capture on its document. Submit and reset keep their native cancellation and ordering.

requestSubmit(submitter) performs native validation and honors the real submitter’s name/value and overrides. submit() bypasses validation and the submit event. Neither runs asynchronous custom checks automatically. Before application work, check both result.status === "valid" and result.current.

FormItemGi retains the existing two-column .m-form-grid CSS layout, full-row span and 40rem responsive collapse. It does not register or require Grid. Native fieldsets keep their border and legend exceptions. Public --m-form-* geometry/color tokens, theme scopes, RTL and forced colors remain CSS-owned.
