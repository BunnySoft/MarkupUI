If the application later supplies a real QR image
  • Supply a trusted, independently verified original asset; this recipe neither generates it nor proves what it encodes.
  • Keep the full verified image, including its quiet zone. Do not crop, recolor, distort or cover it with a logo and assume it remains scannable.
  • Author appropriate dimensions and alt text, with the exact readable payload nearby. Link only a separately approved destination.
  • Verify the actual decoded payload and target scanners, display sizes and print output in the application. Native image display is not encoding or scan verification.

Browsers display supplied images but do not provide a general native QR encoder. BarcodeDetector, where available, is a decoder, not an encoder. This page does not invoke it or request camera, upload or clipboard access.
