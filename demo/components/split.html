Basic horizontal split

Side-by-side panes separated by an interactive resize handle.

Primary Pane Content for the first pane. Secondary Pane Content for the second pane.

Vertical split

Top and bottom panes divided by a horizontal separator handle.

Top Pane Content positioned above the separator. Bottom Pane Content positioned below the separator.

Configured size and constraints

Initial 30% pane size bounded between min 0.2 and max 0.8.

30% Pane Cannot shrink below 20% or exceed 80%. Flexible Pane Adapts to available space.

Disabled split

Resizing handle is inert and non-interactive.

Disabled Left Handle cannot be dragged or adjusted. Disabled Right Content remains accessible.
